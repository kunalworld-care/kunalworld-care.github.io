About Kunalji Nursing Care Service

Care from a nurse who works with his hands and his heart

Kunalji Nursing Care Service is run by Kunal Kumar, a trained bedside nurse based in Jalandhar, Punjab. He has spent his career caring for patients at the bedside - the quiet, skilled, everyday work that helps families breathe easier.

When you call Kunalji Nursing Care Service, you speak directly with the nurse who will care for your loved one. No agencies, no middlemen.
